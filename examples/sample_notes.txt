Machine Learning Basics
Supervised learning trains a model using labeled examples (inputs paired with correct outputs).
Classification predicts categories, such as spam versus not spam.
Regression predicts numeric values, such as a house price.
Unsupervised learning finds structure in unlabeled data, such as clusters.
